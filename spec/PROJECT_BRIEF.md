# Product Brief: VIỆT NAM ĐA TẦNG

Product: Vietnamese one-page interactive educational documentary for FPT University course MLN131, Scientific Socialism, Topic 7 (course Chapter 5). The assignment is humanities/social-science; it is **not** a technology landing page or a dashboard.

Audience: students and lecturers; smartphone users equal priority to desktop audiences. Use for live group presentation and self-study. No login, payment, analytics account, database or CMS required.

## Problem
Explain social structure and social-class structure, three patterns of change in the socialist transitional period, and the **position, role and contribution** of six featured Vietnamese social forces with clear source attribution.

## Scenes and features
1. Intro: 'VIỆT NAM ĐA TẦNG' human-centered documentary hero and scroll invitation.
2. Definition: social structure vs social-class structure; key relationship triad and explanatory interaction.
3. Three patterns: compact scrollytelling with examples and distinction between theory/empirical reality.
4. Six profiles: worker, farmer, intellectual, entrepreneur, women and youth; full accessible position/role/contribution.
5. Collaboration: illustrative rice value chain with interactive steps; alliance note, limited to scope.
6. Retain and test: 3 takeaways, five checked quiz questions and links to cited materials.

Interaction: chapter nav, scroll progress with accessible label, text-size legibility, keyboard-operable explainers and quiz, source popovers / inline references, mobile nav, visible credits. Respect reduced motion.

Deliverables: functioning local Next.js + TS + Tailwind app; content and sources; real screenshots and QA test logs; run/build docs; no deployment unless authorized.

## Approval strategy
G0 confirm academic materials; G1 audited content; G2 design direction; G3 build; G4 visual/mobile QA; G5 release gate. Resolve ordinary details independently and record decisions; ask for approval only on destructive action, paid resources, publication or changing academic substance.

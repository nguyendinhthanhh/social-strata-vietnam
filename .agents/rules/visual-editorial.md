# Editorial art direction rule
- Art direction: contemporary documentary / visual journalism / cultural exhibition; use real human-centered imagery and carefully composed typographic scale.
- Starting palette: Carbon `#151719`, Chalk `#F1F0EA`, Vermilion `#D73732` (large accents only), accessible dark red `#A62E2D` for normal-size links/text on chalk, Citron `#C6D56B` sparingly on dark backgrounds. Verify every actual pair against WCAG; don't assume all combinations are accessible.
- NO identical six round cards, stock-photo spam, decorative sparkles, glassmorphism, gratuitous 3D, hover-only information, fake charts.
- Typography must handle Vietnamese diacritics gracefully. Different photo art direction on mobile: deliberate portrait crops and narrative ordering, not squeezed desktop.
- Design must be validated by real screenshots, not ASCII wireframes.
